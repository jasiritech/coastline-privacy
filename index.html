1. Privacy Policy (English)

COASTLINE Studio built COASTLINE: Life of the Streets (com.coastline.lifeofthestreets) as an open-world 3D coastal city driving, racing, and multiplayer action game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our Android, Windows, or Web applications and our game server.

A. Information We Handle

  • Account & Gameplay Profile (Optional): You may play offline or as a Guest without creating an account. If you choose to register or play online, we store your chosen username, hashed authentication PIN (never stored in plain text), in-game cash/bank balances, owned vehicles/outfits, race wins, and mission progress.
  • Real-Time Multiplayer & Chat: While connected to the Racing Club multiplayer server, your in-game position, vehicle state, race checkpoints, and text chat messages are relayed in real time to nearby players in your session.
  • Microphone / Push-to-Talk Audio (RECORD_AUDIO, MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Audio is accessed only when you actively hold the optional Push-to-Talk button in multiplayer mode. Voice frames are streamed transiently to players in the live session and are never recorded to disk, analyzed for profiling, or shared with advertisers.
  • Network & OTA Updates (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to check server status, download official over-the-air (.pck) game updates, sync multiplayer races, and optionally initiate external wallet/payment actions.
  • Optional Payment Transactions: If you use optional top-up or racing wallet features via our third-party payment gateway partner (Abliner), only transaction reference IDs and settlement statuses are stored for ledger integrity. We never collect or store mobile-money PINs, bank passwords, or credit card numbers.

B. Information We Do NOT Collect

Zero Background Tracking: COASTLINE does not collect precise GPS/location data, contacts, photos, camera video, SMS/call logs, advertising IDs (IDFA/AAID) for behavioral ads, or third-party ad networks. We never sell user data.

C. Data Security & Retention

Account credentials use cryptographic hashing and signed JWT session tokens. Gameplay progression and server logs are kept only as long as needed to run multiplayer races, prevent cheating, and maintain your player account, or until you request deletion.

D. Children's Privacy

COASTLINE is designed for players aged 13 and older (Teen / 12+ content). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 provided account details, contact khatibua04@gmail.com for immediate removal.

2. Sera ya Faragha (Kiswahili)

Studio ya COASTLINE Studio imetengeneza mchezo wa COASTLINE: Life of the Streets (com.coastline.lifeofthestreets) kwa simu za Android na kompyuta. Sera hii inafafanua jinsi taarifa zako zinavyolindwa na kutumika ndani ya mchezo na seva yetu.

A. Taarifa Zinazotumika Ndani ya Mchezo

  • Akaunti na Maendeleo ya Mchezo (Hiari): Unaweza kucheza nje ya mtandao (Offline) au kama Mgeni (Guest). Ukijisajili mtandaoni, tunahifadhi jina ulilochagua (username), PIN iliyofungwa kwa usalama (hashed PIN), salio la mchezo, magari/mavazi uliyonunua, na maendeleo ya misheni na mbio.
  • Multiplayer ya Moja kwa Moja: Unapounganishwa kwenye seva ya Racing Club, nafasi yako ndani ya mji, gari lako, na ujumbe wa chat hutumwa kwa muda huo huo kwa wachezaji wengine walioko mtandaoni.
  • Sauti ya Mic (RECORD_AUDIO, MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Maikrofoni hutumika pekee pale mchezaji anapobonyeza na kushikilia kitufe cha Shikilia Mic [B] (Push-to-Talk). Sauti hupitishwa moja kwa moja kwa wachezaji waliopo kwenye chumba cha mchezo na hairekodiwi wala kuhifadhiwa kwenye seva.
  • Mtandao (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Hutumika kupakua maboresho mapya ya mchezo (OTA updates), kuunganisha Racing Club multiplayer, na kuthibitisha miamala ya hiari.

B. Taarifa Ambazo HATUKUSANYI

Mchezo huu HAUKUSANYI eneo lako halisi (GPS), anwani zako (Contacts), picha zako, kamera, SMS, wala matangazo ya kufuatilia watumiaji. Hatuuzi wala kusambaza taarifa za wachezaji kwa wahusika wengine.

3. Account & Data Deletion / Maombi ya Kufuta Akaunti

In accordance with Google Play policies, you may request permanent deletion of your registered COASTLINE online account and associated progression data at any time:

How to Delete Your Account (Jinsi ya Kufuta Akaunti):
  1. Send an email to khatibua04@gmail.com with the subject line: COASTLINE Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include your registered in-game Username so we can verify account ownership.
  3. All registered profile records, progression stats, and session tokens will be permanently erased within 7 business days (confirming via reply email).

Guest & Offline Play: Local save data is stored only on your device and is automatically removed when you clear app data or uninstall COASTLINE.

Changes to This Policy: Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. For any privacy questions, contact COASTLINE Studio at khatibua04@gmail.com.
